# RL & AI for Autonomous Vehicles — A Technical Learning Journal

Welcome to my personal repository documenting an ongoing journey through **Reinforcement Learning (RL)** and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** in the context of **autonomous vehicle systems**. As a simulation engineer working at the intersection of motorsport and advanced vehicle dynamics, I’m using this space to explore, reflect, and share insights from my deep dive into intelligent mobility.

## 🎯 Purpose

This journal is designed to:

- Consolidate technical learnings from papers, experiments, and simulations  
- Translate complex methodologies into practical, reproducible workflows  
- Bridge academic research with real-world engineering applications  
- Invite discussion and collaboration with fellow researchers and practitioners  

## 🧪 Topics of Interest

- RL for control, planning, and decision-making  
- Trajectory prediction using LSTM-MDN, GANs, and diffusion models  
- Multi-agent systems and emergent behavior  
- Simulation-based validation and benchmarking  
- Integration with Simulink, Python, CARLA, and vehicle dynamics tools  

## 📘 Format

Each post includes:

- A focused technical theme or research question  
- Key takeaways and implementation notes  
- Links to relevant papers, datasets, and tools  
- Reflections on challenges, assumptions, and next steps

This repo is part of a broader effort to document and share learnings as I build toward advanced research and innovation in autonomous mobility. If you're working in related areas or have feedback, I’d love to connect.
